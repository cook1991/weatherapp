# SkyCast — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 12 July 2026_

SkyCast ("the app") is a free weather app. It has **no user accounts, no ads, and
no analytics or tracking**. This policy explains the little data the app touches
and where it goes.

## What the app collects

- **Approximate location — only when you ask for it.** If you tap "Use my
  location," the app reads your device's *approximate* (coarse) location once to
  look up local weather. The app does not request precise GPS location and does
  not track your location in the background.
- **City searches you type.** When you search for a city, the text you enter is
  sent to the weather provider's geocoding service to find matching places.

The app does **not** collect your name, email, contacts, device identifiers, or
any advertising ID.

## How your location is used, and who receives it

To turn a location into a forecast, coordinates are sent to third-party services.
We do not operate any servers of our own and store nothing about you remotely:

- **Open-Meteo** (open-meteo.com) — receives coordinates (or a searched city
  name) to return the weather forecast. See Open-Meteo's privacy terms.
- **Your device's system geocoder** (provided by the device / Google Play
  Services) — used first to turn coordinates into a city name.
- **BigDataCloud** (bigdatacloud.com) — used only as a fallback when the device
  geocoder is unavailable, to turn coordinates into a city name.
- **RainViewer** (rainviewer.com) — provides the precipitation-radar imagery. The
  app requests radar map tiles for the area shown on the radar map (which is
  centered on your location), so the map region you view is sent to RainViewer.
- **MapTiler** (maptiler.com) — provides the base map tiles beneath the radar (map
  data © OpenStreetMap). As with any map, the tiles for the area you view are
  requested from MapTiler's servers.

Location is used solely to provide weather information. It is **never** sold, and
**never** used for advertising or profiling.

## What is stored, and where

The app saves two things **locally on your device only**: your last-viewed
location and your temperature-unit preference (°C/°F). This never leaves the
device and is removed if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information.
