## Full description (max 4000 chars)
```
SkyCast is a clean, fast weather app that respects you. No ads. No tracking. No sign-up. Just the forecast you came for.

Tap once for the weather where you are, or search any city in the world. SkyCast shows current conditions on a temperature dial, plus everything you need to plan your day.

WHAT YOU GET
• Current temperature, "feels like", and conditions at a glance
• Humidity, wind speed and direction, and chance of precipitation
• Sunrise and sunset times
• Next-24-hours hourly forecast
• 7-day forecast with daily high/low and rain chance
• °C / °F toggle — your choice is remembered
• Search any city, or use your location for local weather

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No ads, ever
• No analytics, no tracking, no advertising IDs
• No account and no login
• Your location is used only to fetch weather, never sold or profiled
• Only approximate location is requested — SkyCast never needs precise GPS

BEAUTIFULLY SIMPLE
A dark, easy-to-read interface with a temperature dial and clear typography. No clutter, no pop-ups, no nagging — the weather, and nothing else.

Weather data is provided by Open-Meteo, with precipitation radar by RainViewer over MapTiler maps (map data © OpenStreetMap). SkyCast is free to use.
```
